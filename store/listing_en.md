# Store listing — English

**App name (max 30):**
Satr PDF - Arabic PDF tools

**Short description (max 80):**
Merge, split, compress PDFs and read Arabic text from images. Offline & private.

**Full description:**
Satr is a set of PDF and image tools built for Arabic from the ground up, and it works entirely on your device.

Your files never leave your phone: no uploads, no account, no sign-up. The app doesn't even have internet permission.

Tools:
• Merge PDFs and set the order by dragging
• Split by page ranges or every few pages
• Extract, reorder, rotate, delete and add blank pages
• Compress PDFs with three levels and see the size before/after
• PDF to JPG/PNG, and images (including iPhone HEIC photos) to PDF
• Watermarks with correctly shaped Arabic text, or an image
• Page numbers, including Eastern Arabic digits (١٢٣)
• Drawn signature with your finger or from a photo of your signature
• Extract the text of a PDF, with Arabic letter order repaired
• Read Arabic and English text from images (OCR), save as text or Word
• PDF viewer with search and night mode
• My files: Arabic-aware search, favourites and a trash

Arabic right-to-left interface plus English, light and dark themes.

Honest note: a drawn signature is an image of your signature, not a certified digital signature. OCR accuracy depends on image quality.
